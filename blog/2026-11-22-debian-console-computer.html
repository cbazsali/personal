2026-11-22 - Debian console computer

Brother mine! Our school upgraded our Chromebooks this month and gave us the offer to buy our old ones for $50. I took them up on their offer, and this weekend I spent much of my time converting it into a Linux CLI machine–that is, Linux with only the command-line-interface. No, graphics, no windows, just direct access to the console.

Not suprisingly, there are some serious limitations, but that’s the point. When I use this machine, I have no choice but to use all text commands. I can write in my wiki, update my website and blog, check RSS feeds, read and write emails, access BBS’s, and even access the web through text-only browsers. So far, that’s about as much as I’ve done. I’ve spend most of my time today tweaking color schemes. Almost there.

Another serious limitation is I can’t watch or listen to your compositions on it. I may find a way to listen, but not watch. For that I’ll have to switch to the Ubuntu laptop.
