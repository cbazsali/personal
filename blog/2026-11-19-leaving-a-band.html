2026-11-19 - Leaving a band

I recently left a band I’ve been in for more than 20 years, over some serious disagreements. It was a sad way to end something that has been an important part of my life for so long, but the differences are personal and irreconcilable.

I miss very much being in the band, especially when I am reminded of how much I enjoy spending time with them. It was a great social outlet for me, but it can be helped.

Drumming was not a major musical priority for me. I started out as a drummer, but guitar and especially banjo took over as my primary musical passions. I think I’m a good drummer, but I’m not great. I think I am good enough for the role I played in the band, but that was part of the aforeseaid disagreement.

C’est la vie.
